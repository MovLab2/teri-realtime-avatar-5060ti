Welcome. What you are watching is a real-time AI avatar running entirely on a single consumer GPU. Your voice or text goes to an AI model which thinks, replies, and generates speech. That speech drives a diffusion model that creates this face frame-by-frame, lip-synced in real-time. It streams over the web through WebRTC, so anyone can join from any browser, powered by SoulX FlashHead, OpenAI, and LiveKit. This is what local real-time AI avatars look like.
